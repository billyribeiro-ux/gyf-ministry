# Grow Your Faith Ministries website audit

November 21, 2026

The audit covered the public ministry pages, journal index and archives, articles, shared navigation and footer, error states, search feeds, and the admin sign-in and access redirects. The redesign follows three ways visitors engage with the ministry. They can ask for prayer or practical help, explore Friday ministry and purpose restoration, or support community outreach.

| Area                                        | Audit findings and changes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Homepage                                    | The original opening put a long mission statement and animated metrics ahead of support. An edge-to-edge portrait leads into a direct invitation to ask for prayer. The three ministry programs sit together, with archive photos, Courtney’s story, and the ministry blog further down.                                                                           |
| Navigation and footer                       | The previous eight-link navigation was difficult to scan. The ministry programs now have a dedicated menu, prayer and giving are easy to reach, and the footer groups links by visitor intent. The holiday banner uses the recurring outreach window.                                                                                                              |
| About, programs, resources, and partnership | Repeated page layouts buried the most useful action. Editorial mastheads and page-specific content bring booking, sponsorship, assistance, and resources forward. The holiday page gives the campaign dates and goal without an unverified progress figure.                                                                                                        |
| Prayer, giving, and purpose restoration     | The prayer selector could discard drafts and relied on JavaScript. The revised selector retains drafts when switching and supports direct links. Forms report storage errors, giving uses explicit pledge choices, and the Seven-Day Mustard Seed Reflection Guide is available as a download.                                                                     |
| Blog                                        | The index repeated featured posts, archive layouts diverged, and the article progress bar included unrelated page content. The journal now has a photo-led masthead, clearer search and empty results, reusable archives, an article-only progress indicator, and a direct route to prayer.                                                                        |
| Motion and accessibility                    | The original page entrances could temporarily hide content, and both galleries rotated automatically. The site now uses an opening portrait push-in, staggered copy, and a scroll-triggered aperture and settle on selected photographs. Copy renders immediately, motion respects the visitor’s reduced-motion setting, and the galleries advance by choice.      |
| Photography                                 | The hero, ministry cards, outreach galleries, and journal used several different crop ratios. Feature photos now use 4:3 frames that match within each row. The bundled archive was color-graded. The homepage uses a labeled AI-assisted retouch of the original portrait and responsive image widths. Journal uploads receive a visual treatment in the browser. |
| Metadata and recovery                       | The sitemap and feed now use the configured public origin. A new site-wide error page helps visitors find their way home or reach prayer and support. The blog keeps its own recovery page.                                                                                                                                                                        |

## Verification

- Chromium checked 28 routes and endpoints, with five viewport widths from 320 to 1440 pixels on HTML pages. The review included Axe scans, keyboard navigation, gallery controls, and the public error pages.
- Photo checks verified matching 4:3 frames, aligned gallery edges, and successful image decoding at five viewport widths from 320 to 1440 pixels.
- Browser interaction checks covered prayer draft retention, callback validation, sponsorship selection, the reflection guide download, journal search, and the article reading controls.
- The production build, Svelte diagnostics, lint, and 157 automated tests passed.

## Production follow-through

Ministry forms capture requests and sponsorship pledges. Live intake requires a persistent PostgreSQL database, applied migrations, and a staff process for reviewing new submissions. Payment collection and newsletter delivery need their own integrations. Verification of authenticated admin screens was limited to existing automated component tests. The browser audit used local data and unauthenticated access.
